## Subtitle

Private friend group hub

## Description

GSL is a private hub for one invite-only friend group — not a public social network.

Inside the group you can:

- Follow a shared activity feed
- Chat in group threads
- Plan meetups with scheduling polls
- Share event photo albums
- Track who hosts each month

Access is for invited members only. Push notifications are optional (messages, polls, tags, and host reminders). Photos and other group content stay within the private group.
