GSL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

GSL is a private, invite-only app for a small friend group. It is not a public social network. This page explains what data the app handles, in plain language.

What the app does

Members can use:

  • a shared activity feed
  • group chat
  • plans and scheduling polls
  • photo albums
  • monthly host rotation

Account and membership

Access is limited to invited group members. We store account details needed to sign in and show your profile in the group (for example display name, optional contact email, avatar, and notification preferences). Membership and roles are managed by the group admin.

Content you create

Messages, poll responses, host assignments, feed posts, and photos you upload are stored so other members of the same private group can see them. Photos and similar media are uploaded by members for shared group use.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may send pushes about messages, polls, tags, or host reminders. You can change notification preferences in the app (and in your device settings).

Where data is stored

App data is stored in Supabase (database, auth, storage, and related backend services). Device tokens for push may also be stored so notifications can be delivered.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not run ads in the app.
  • We do not share group content with the public; the app is for the invited friend group only.

Retention and deletion

Data stays while you are a member and the group keeps using the app. A group admin can remove accounts or content. If you want your account or data removed, contact the group admin (see Support).

Children

GSL is intended for the private friend group’s adult members. It is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the group admin, or open an issue on the project repository — github.com/tvlins-git/gsl/issues.
